Under the Employment Act, a contract that lasts more than three months, or that requires work to be done over a period longer than three months, must be in writing.

### What it should cover

- Names of employer and employee, and the job title or description.
- Place and date of work, and how pay is calculated and paid.
- Hours of work, leave, and notice or termination terms.

### If there is no written contract

Your rights under the Act still apply. Pay slips, messages and bank records can help show the terms you worked under.

### Disputes

Claims such as unfair termination are heard by the Employment and Labour Relations Court. Many matters start with the labour office.
