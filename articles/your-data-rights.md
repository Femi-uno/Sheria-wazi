The Data Protection Act, 2019 governs how organisations collect and use personal data. It is enforced by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

### Your rights

- To be told how your data will be used.
- To access the data an organisation holds about you.
- To object to certain uses of it.
- To have wrong data corrected, and in some cases deleted.

### If an organisation ignores you

You can complain to the ODPC, which can investigate and order a remedy.

### For site owners

Organisations that collect personal data, including through contact forms or mailing lists, may need to register with the ODPC.
