The law that applies depends on what you rent. Your written tenancy agreement matters most, but some statutes limit what a landlord can do.

### Homes

Some residential premises are covered by the Rent Restriction Act, with disputes going to the Rent Restriction Tribunal. Whether it applies depends on the type of premises and the rent.

### Shops and business premises

The Landlord and Tenant (Shops, Hotels and Catering Establishments) Act protects many business tenants. Disputes go to the Business Premises Rent Tribunal.

### Practical points

- Keep a signed copy of the agreement and receipts for every payment.
- A landlord generally has to follow a lawful notice process. Locking you out or removing your goods without one is likely to be unlawful.
