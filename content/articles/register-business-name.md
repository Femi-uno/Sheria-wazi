A business name is the simplest way to trade under a name that is not your own. It suits sole proprietors and partnerships. If you want limited liability, you register a company instead.

### Business name

- Search and reserve your preferred name with the Business Registration Service (BRS), which runs on the eCitizen platform.
- Submit the owner details, business address and nature of business.
- Pay the prescribed fee and receive a certificate of registration.

### Company

A private company is a separate legal person, and its owners are generally not personally liable for its debts. It has more filing duties, including annual returns and beneficial ownership information.

### Tax and licences

Registration does not replace tax registration with the Kenya Revenue Authority or any county business permit your activity needs.
